Privacy

Privacy Policy

The Naija Marxists respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. What We Collect

We collect the following information when you subscribe to our mailing list:

  • Your name (first and last)
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if you choose to provide it)
  • Your subscription status (subscribed/unsubscribed)
  • The date and time you subscribed or unsubscribed

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Send you updates on new articles, statements, and events
  • Keep you informed about the work of The Naija Marxists
  • Maintain a record of our mailing list subscribers

We do not share, sell, or trade your personal information with third parties.

3. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us
  • Update your email preferences

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Your information is stored securely in our Supabase database. Only authorised members of The Naija Marxists have access to this data.

5. Cookies

We do not use cookies for the mailing list or subscription functionality. If we introduce analytics in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect that.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, or if you wish to request deletion of your data, please contact us at:

Email: contact@thenaijamarxists.org
